SIMULATED ENVIRONMENT

Success that holds up

Estimated pass^k across tasks

What is pass^k? The probability that all k attempts succeed, averaged across tasks. Estimated as C(s, k) / C(n, k), where s is successful trials and n is total trials for a task. Every selected task needs at least k trials; otherwise the estimate is unavailable. Success requires both the expected final state and policy compliance.
