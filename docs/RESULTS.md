The authentication-only agent reaches correct final states because tool guards reject prohibited actions. Its attempted violations lower joint success. This demonstrates why state-only scoring can hide bad decisions in a guarded environment. The policy-aware control is designed against these fixtures, so its perfect result is not evidence of generalization.
